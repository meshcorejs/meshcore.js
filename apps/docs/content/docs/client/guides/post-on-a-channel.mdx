## What happens

`client.channels.get()` finds a channel by name or by slot index in the radio's table. The bot answers
if the channel is missing instead of failing.

`channel.send()` accepts a string or a `MessageBuilder`. A plain string over the budget throws
`MessageTooLongError`. The builder with `setOverflow('split')` sends up to three numbered parts instead.

The message goes through the send queue. Transmissions are two seconds apart and the parts of one message
stay together.

<Callout type="warn">
A channel accepts at most **10 messages per 5 minutes**; beyond that `channel.send()` rejects with
`RateLimitError`, nothing is queued. Sending on the **Public** channel is refused outright with
`PublicChannelError` — see [A well-behaved bot](./a-well-behaved-bot).
</Callout>
