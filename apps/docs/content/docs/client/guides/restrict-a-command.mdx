## What happens

The role lives under `roles/`, the command under `commands/`. A role has a priority, permissions and members. Members are public keys, or key prefixes of twelve
characters or more. The bot never stores them. They come from your own source, here an environment
variable.

`setRequiredPermissions()` on the command is checked before the handler runs. Without the permission the
person gets `Permission denied`.

A channel author cannot be verified. A command with a required permission is refused on a channel, silently —
no reply on the air, only `commandDenied` with `channelUntrusted`. Run it in a DM instead. See
[Channels are untrusted](../permissions/channels-are-untrusted) for why.

## Scopes

`setScope()` takes one or more of `'dm' | 'channel' | 'public'` — where the command may be triggered. It
defaults to `'dm'` and `'channel'`: the firmware's Public channel is never included unless a command opts in
explicitly, `setScope('public')` (alongside the others if it should answer everywhere). See
[A well-behaved bot](./a-well-behaved-bot) for why Public is opt-in and what else the framework refuses by
default.
