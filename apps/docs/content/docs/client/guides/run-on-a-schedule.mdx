## What happens

A job takes either `setInterval(seconds)` or `setCron(expression, { timezone })`. Files under `jobs/` are
loaded at login. Jobs start when the bot becomes ready and stop with `destroy()`.

`setRunOnStart()` also runs the job once at startup. A scheduled run is skipped while the radio is
disconnected.

A handler that throws is reported through the `error` event and the job keeps its schedule. From a DM,
`/jobs run|pause|resume <name>` controls it.
