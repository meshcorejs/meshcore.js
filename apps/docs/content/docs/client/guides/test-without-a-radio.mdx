The owner announces, the bot posts. A stranger tries, the bot refuses. Both checked in a test, without a
radio.

<include lang="ts" meta='title="announce.test.ts"'>../../../../snippets/client/guides/test-without-a-radio.test.ts#test</include>

## What happens

`createTestClient()` logs a real `Client` in on a `FakeRadio` and loads the bricks of a folder.
`fakeContact()` and `fakeChannel()` fill the radio's tables.

`dm()` sends a message as a contact and returns what the bot answered. `sentTo()` returns what the bot
put on a channel.

Time is simulated. `advanceTime('6h')` fires the jobs due in between. `destroy()` releases everything.
